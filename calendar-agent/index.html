Calendar Agent for Nikos Mylonopoulos

Calendar Agent is a booking page. Students, colleagues and other contacts of Nikos Mylonopoulos use it to choose a time for a meeting with him, without an exchange of emails.

How it works

  1. The visitor states how long the meeting should be and how to meet: by video call, by phone, or in person.
  2. The page shows the times when Nikos is free. It shows only start times, never what else is in his calendar.
  3. The visitor enters a name, an email address and a short topic, and confirms.
  4. The meeting is added to Nikos's Google Calendar. The visitor receives a calendar invitation, with a video link when the meeting is online, and a confirmation email with a private link to change or cancel the meeting. Nikos receives an email about the booking.

Use of Google data

Calendar Agent connects to one Google account only: Nikos Mylonopoulos's own. It reads his calendar to find free times, adds the meetings that visitors book, sends him an email about each booking, and sends the person who booked one confirmation email. It does not access any visitor's Google account.
