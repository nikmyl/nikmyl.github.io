Calendar Agent for Nikos Mylonopoulos

Calendar Agent is a booking page. Students, colleagues and other contacts of Nikos Mylonopoulos use it to choose a time for a meeting with him, without an exchange of emails.

How it works

  1. The visitor states how long the meeting should be and how to meet: by video call, by phone, or in person.
  2. The page shows the times when Nikos is free. It shows only start times, never what else is in his calendar.
  3. The visitor confirms their email address with a 6-digit code sent to it (once every 12 hours in the same browser), then enters a name and a short topic, and confirms the meeting.
  4. Most bookings are confirmed at once. A request beyond the usual limits (more than five bookings in a day from one address, or more than ten guests) waits for Nikos's approval, and the time is kept meanwhile.
  5. The meeting is added to Nikos's Google Calendar. The visitor receives a calendar invitation, with a video link when the meeting is online, and a confirmation email with a private link to change or cancel the meeting. Nikos receives an email about the booking.

Use of Google data

Calendar Agent connects to one Google account only: Nikos Mylonopoulos's own. It reads his calendar to find free times and adds the meetings that visitors book. From his Gmail it sends: the code that confirms a visitor's address; one confirmation to the person who booked; emails to Nikos about bookings; and, for the few requests that need his approval, the outcome to the person who asked. It does not access any visitor's Google account.
