Calendar Agent: privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026 (email codes for every visitor; approval of requests beyond the usual limits).

This policy explains what information Calendar Agent collects when someone books a meeting with Nikos Mylonopoulos, why, where it is kept, for how long, and how to have it corrected or deleted.

Who is responsible

Nikos Mylonopoulos, Athens, Greece, runs Calendar Agent and decides how the information is used. Contact: nikmyl@gmail.com.

What is collected from visitors, and why

InformationWhy it is needed
Name, email address and a short topicTo identify the meeting, and to send the calendar invitation to the visitor.
A one-time code sent to the visitor's email addressTo confirm that the address belongs to the person booking, so that nobody can book in someone else's name. The code expires after 10 minutes; Cloudflare keeps only a scrambled form of it, and of the address, for at most 12 hours.
The chosen time, length and format of the meetingTo create the meeting in Nikos's calendar.
An address, for an in-person meeting outside Alba Graduate Business SchoolTo allow for travel time and to tell Nikos where to go.
A meeting link supplied by the visitor (for example Microsoft Teams)To include it in the invitation.
Email addresses of other people the visitor chooses to inviteTo send them the calendar invitation. The visitor should invite only people who expect it.
The visitor's internet (IP) address, in a scrambled formTo limit how many codes can be requested from one address, which protects against misuse. It is deleted automatically within an hour.

Visitors give this information in order to arrange a meeting they have asked for. A visitor who does not want to give it can ask for a meeting by email instead.

Where the information is kept

  • Google Calendar and Gmail (Google): the meeting is stored as an event in Nikos's calendar. Nikos receives an email about it, and the person who booked receives a confirmation email, sent from Nikos's Gmail, with a private link to change or cancel the meeting.
  • Cloudflare: runs the booking page, and keeps short-lived technical records (the scrambled address above, and the one-time links for changing or cancelling a booking). These records expire automatically.
  • Zoom: only when the visitor chooses a Zoom meeting; the meeting is created in Nikos's Zoom account.

The information is not sold, not shared for marketing, and not used for advertising. The page uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

How long it is kept

A booked meeting stays in Nikos's calendar as a record of the meeting, as any other meeting in his calendar does. A visitor can ask at any time for their details to be removed from it.

Use of Google user data

Calendar Agent connects to one Google account only: Nikos Mylonopoulos's own. It reads the events in his calendar to find free times and creates the events that visitors book. From his Gmail it sends only: the code that confirms a visitor's address; one confirmation to the person who made each booking; emails to Nikos himself about bookings; and, for requests that need his approval, the outcome to the person who asked. It sends no other email. It never reads or changes any visitor's Google account. Data obtained from Google is used only to provide these functions. It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. Calendar Agent's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you can ask to see the information held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to its use. Write to nikmyl@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
